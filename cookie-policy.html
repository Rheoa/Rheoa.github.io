1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They help websites remember your preferences, analyze usage, and improve your experience.

Local Storage is similar but stores data directly in your browser. Unlike cookies, local storage data is not sent to the server with every request. World Discovery primarily uses localStorage rather than traditional cookies.

📌 Key Difference: localStorage stays in your browser and is not automatically sent to our servers. This means we store less data on our side.

2. What We Store & Why

We use localStorage for the following purposes:

Name Purpose Type Duration
visitorId Unique identifier to distinguish you from other visitors. Used for analytics and click counting. Functional Persistent

We do not use:

  • Third-party tracking cookies (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel)
  • Advertising cookies
  • Session replay or heatmap tools

3. How We Use Your Data

The data we store locally is used for:

  • Analytics — Understanding which articles are popular and how visitors navigate our site.
  • Personalization — Showing you relevant affiliate links based on your country.
  • Performance — Caching affiliate links to make pages load faster.
  • Consent Management — Remembering your cookie preference so we don't ask you repeatedly.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Managing Your Preferences

You have full control over your data stored locally. Here's how you can manage it:

5.1 Accept or Decline

When you first visit World Discovery, you'll see a cookie banner. You can choose to:

  • Accept — Allows us to store functional and performance data.
  • Decline — Only essential data (consent preference) will be stored.

5.2 Clear Local Storage

You can clear all localStorage data at any time through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Clear browsing data → Select "Cookies and other site data"
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Clear browsing data

Learn more about clearing localStorage

5.3 Browser Settings

You can also configure your browser to block or limit localStorage usage. Note that some features may not work properly if you do this.

6. Consent

We ask for your consent before storing non-essential data in your browser. Here's how it works:

  • When you first visit, a banner appears asking you to accept or decline.
  • If you accept, we store your preference (cookiesAccepted: true) and enable all features.
  • If you decline, we store your preference (cookiesAccepted: false) and only store essential data.
  • You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

We do not store or process personal data without your consent.

  • Your continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
