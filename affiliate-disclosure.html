1. What Are Affiliate Links?

Affiliate links are special URLs that track when a user clicks through from one website to another. When you click an affiliate link on World Discovery and make a purchase or booking, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

This is how we support the site and continue to create free, high-quality travel content.

📌 The Short Version: We may earn a commission if you buy something through our links. It doesn't cost you extra.

2. Our Affiliate Partners

We work with the following affiliate programs:

🛒 Amazon Associates
Amazon product links (books, gear, travel essentials)
🌐 Pelago
Tours, experiences, and activities worldwide
🌏 KKday
Activities, tours, and travel experiences
📚 Other Partners
Additional partners as disclosed in individual articles

We only recommend products, services, and destinations that we genuinely believe offer value to our readers.

3. How Affiliate Links Work

When you click an affiliate link on World Discovery, here's what happens:

  1. You click a link with an affiliate identifier (e.g., amazon_tour, pelago_tour, or kkday_tour).
  2. Our system dynamically rewrites the link based on your country to ensure the best regional offer.
  3. You're redirected to the partner's website where you can make a purchase or booking.
  4. If you make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

The link rewriting is done automatically in the background — you don't need to do anything special.

4. How to Identify Affiliate Links

We make it easy to spot affiliate links:

  • 📌 Label: Affiliate links are clearly labeled with [Affiliate] or 🛒 near the link text.
  • 📄 Disclosure: Each article includes a full disclosure at the bottom.
  • 🔗 Data Attribute: All affiliate links use the data-affiliate-key attribute for tracking.

If you ever see a link to a product or service on our site, it's likely an affiliate link unless explicitly stated otherwise.

5. Editorial Independence

Our content is independent. Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial decisions.

  • We write about places, products, and services we find genuinely interesting.
  • We do not accept payment in exchange for positive reviews.
  • Our recommendations are based on research, expertise, and genuine interest.

📌 We Value Your Trust: We only recommend what we believe is worth your time and money.

6. Your Responsibility

While we strive to provide accurate and helpful information, please note:

  • Verify before booking: Always check current prices, availability, and terms directly with the merchant.
  • Travel conditions change: Check official travel advisories and local conditions before your trip.
  • Read the fine print: Each affiliate partner has its own terms and policies.

We are not responsible for any decisions you make based on our content or affiliate recommendations.

7. Commission & Pricing

Important things to know about commissions and pricing:

  • No extra cost: The price you pay is the same whether you use our link or go directly to the merchant.
  • Commission rates vary: Different programs offer different commission rates (typically 1-10% of the purchase price).
  • Not all clicks earn commissions: Commissions are only earned when a purchase or booking is completed.
  • We cannot control pricing: Prices, availability, and terms are set by the merchant, not by us.
