1. Information We Collect

We collect limited information to operate and improve World Discovery. Depending on how you interact with our site, we may collect:

1.1 Automatically Collected

  • IP Address — Used for geolocation (country detection) and fraud prevention.
  • Browser & Device Information — Browser type, version, operating system, and screen size.
  • Visitor ID — A unique identifier stored in your browser's localStorage to distinguish unique visitors for analytics.
  • Referral Source — The website that referred you to World Discovery.
  • Affiliate Clicks — Which affiliate links you click, when, and which article or product they appeared in.

1.2 Voluntarily Provided

  • Email Address — If you create an admin account or contact us via email.
  • Contact Form Submissions — Any information you provide when reaching out to us.

📌 We do not collect: Your name, phone number, physical address, payment information, or sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: For storing non‑essential data (visitorId, userCountry, affiliate caches) — you can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interest: For analytics, affiliate commission tracking, and fraud prevention — these are essential for operating the site.
  • Legal Obligation: Where required by applicable laws (e.g., responding to legal requests).

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve our content — We use analytics to understand which articles and destinations are most popular.
  • To personalize your experience — Your country code helps us show you relevant affiliate links.
  • To track affiliate commissions — Affiliate click data is used to attribute commissions to our partners.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse — IP addresses and Visitor IDs help us detect unusual activity.
  • To communicate with you — If you contact us, we may use your email to respond.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

World Discovery uses browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store certain data for functionality and performance.

What We Store Locally

  • visitorId — A unique ID to distinguish you from other visitors. Used for analytics and click counting.
  • userCountry — Your detected country code, cached to avoid repeated geolocation lookups.
  • userCountryTimestamp — When your country was last detected (cache expires after 1 hour).
  • affiliate_links_{articleId}_{country} — Cached affiliate link data for faster page loads (expires after 1 hour).
  • cookiesAccepted — Your consent preference for non-essential storage (if you accept our cookie policy).

Note: We do not use third-party tracking cookies (e.g., Google Analytics) at this time. If we add them, we will update this policy and request your consent.

You can clear all localStorage data at any time through your browser settings. Learn how.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Google Fonts — We load the Inter font family from Google Fonts for visual design. When you visit our site, your browser connects to Google's servers and may transmit your IP address. Google processes this data according to its own privacy policy.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — Our database and authentication provider. Your Visitor ID, IP address, country code, affiliate clicks, and (if applicable) admin email are stored in Supabase. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Impact.com — Our affiliate network partner. When you click an affiliate link, Impact.com may collect data to track conversions and commissions. Impact.com Privacy Policy
  • Pexels — We use Pexels for article images. Pexels does not collect personal data through our site. Pexels License
  • Leaflet.js / OpenStreetMap — Used for interactive maps on some articles. OpenStreetMap does not collect personal data through our site. OpenStreetMap Copyright

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted between your browser and our services is encrypted using HTTPS.
  • Supabase, our database provider, implements industry-standard security practices.
  • We do not store sensitive personal data (passwords, payment info, etc.).
  • Admin accounts are protected by secure password authentication.

While we strive to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. Please do not share sensitive information via unsecured channels.

6. Data Retention

  • Click Logs: Retained for a period of 24 months for analytics and affiliate reporting. After this period, data is anonymized (IP addresses removed) and stored in aggregated form only.
  • Visitor ID: Stored in your browser localStorage indefinitely unless you clear it.
  • Admin Account Data: Retained as long as the account is active. You may request deletion at any time.
  • IP Addresses: Retained as part of click logs for the retention period mentioned above.

You have the right to request the deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA / DPDP)

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

If you are in the European Union (GDPR) or United Kingdom (UK GDPR)

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification — Request corrections to inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restrict Processing — Request limited use of your data.
  • Right to Data Portability — Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Right to Object — Object to processing for certain purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — Withdraw your consent at any time.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in your country of residence, place of work, or where an alleged infringement of data protection law occurred.

  • EU Supervisory Authorities: List of EU Data Protection Authorities
  • UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk

If you are in California (CCPA)

  • Right to Know — Request disclosure of personal data collected, used, or shared.
  • Right to Delete — Request deletion of personal data.
  • Right to Opt-Out — Opt-out of the sale of personal data. (We do not sell data.)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

If you are in India (DPDP Act, 2023)

As a Data Fiduciary operating from India, World Discovery complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. We collect and process personal data only for the purposes specified in this policy, with your consent, and for legitimate business interests. You have the right to:

  • Access and rectify your personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Nominate a representative to exercise your rights
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

Our Grievance Officer can be reached at grievance@worlddiscovery.com.

Other Regions

If you are located in Canada (PIPEDA), Brazil (LGPD), Australia (Privacy Act), or other jurisdictions with similar data protection laws, World Discovery applies the same privacy protections described in this policy to all users globally. We honor your rights to access, correct, and delete your personal data regardless of your location.

📧 To exercise your rights, contact us at: hello@worlddiscovery.com

We will respond within 30 days as required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

For users in the EU, the minimum age for consent is 16 (or lower in certain countries). We do not target users under the age of consent.
